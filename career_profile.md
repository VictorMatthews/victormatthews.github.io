# Career Profile Writeup

I am a Senior Software Engineer on the Data Engineering - Foundations team at Jeppesen ForeFlight, where I build the high-integrity pipelines and APIs that transform raw data into critical navigation content for pilots. Operating in a safety-critical domain, I ensure that the data informing aircraft guidance and terminal operations is correct and verifiable, treating reliability as a first-class citizen alongside system throughput.

My expertise spans the aeronautical domain and a diverse tech stack, including Java SpringBoot, Python, React, PostgreSQL, SQLite, CosmosDB, AWS, and Azure.

A highlight of my work is the creation of an automated labeling and deconfliction system for airport map features. This system now automates over 75% of the airport library's labeling process, replacing a labor-intensive manual workflow that had to be repeated every regulatory update cycle. This optimization directly powers Jeppesen's Airport Moving Maps and ForeFlight's flight-deck applications.

I thrive in environments where engineering rigor is paramount and the stakes are high. I am driven by the challenge of building systems where "correctness" is non-negotiable and a small mistake has real-world consequences.
